About India Post

India Post is one of the oldest and most trusted public service institutions in India, serving as the backbone of communication across the country for more than a century. Established in 1854, India Post has connected millions of people through letters, parcels, financial services, and life insurance, building trust in every corner of the nation.

History of India Post

The history of India Post is the story of how communication evolved in India—from handwritten letters carried by foot messengers to one of the largest postal networks in the world. With roots stretching back centuries, India Post has grown into one of India’s most trusted public service institutions.

Unique Post Offices

India Post is home to some of the most unique post offices in the world, each known for its remarkable location, history, or cultural significance. These post offices are more than service centers—they are symbols of India’s diversity, geography, and postal legacy.

These unique post offices reflect the extraordinary reach of India Post—from mountain peaks and floating waters to historic cities and remote islands—showcasing the unmatched diversity of India’s postal network.
